CityRP Server Rules

Welcome to our CityRP community! Please read all rules carefully before playing. Ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse for breaking them. Breaking any rule may result in a warning, kick, or ban depending on severity.

General Rules

  • Stay In Character (IC) — Keep IC and OOC (out of character) separate at all times.

Roleplay Rules

  • RDM (Random Death Match) is Prohibited — You must have valid IC reason to harm another player.
  • RDA (Random Arrest) is Prohibited — Police/gang enforcers need valid IC reason to detain someone.
  • NLR (New Life Rule) — After death, you forget the events leading to it and cannot return to the scene for a set amount of time (typically 5–10 minutes).
  • FailRP is Prohibited — Always act realistically according to your job/character. No random or nonsensical behavior.
Have fun and roleplay creatively! Respect the community and staff team. Rules may be updated at any time — check back regularly. Enjoy your time in the city!
